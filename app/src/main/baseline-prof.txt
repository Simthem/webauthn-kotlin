# Baseline profile for the app's own code.
#
# Compose and the other AndroidX libraries ship profiles of their own, which AGP merges in,
# but nothing covered PQ Vault itself: the passkey list, the view model and the vault core
# ran interpreted until the JIT caught up, which is what made the first scroll after an
# install or an update stutter. The app is small enough to compile ahead of time as a
# whole, so the rules are wildcards rather than a recorded trace that would need a
# Macrobenchmark module and a device to keep up to date.
#
# profileinstaller (already on the classpath through Compose) hands this profile to ART
# for APKs installed outside the Play Store, and ART compiles it during the next idle
# maintenance window. Debuggable builds are never compiled ahead of time and ignore it.
HSPLcom/pqvault/app/**;->**(**)**
HSPLcom/pqvault/core/**;->**(**)**
Lcom/pqvault/app/**;
Lcom/pqvault/core/**;
